ADDITIONAL RETROSPECTIVE UPR QUINTILE AND RANKING-UTILITY ANALYSIS
Version 1; 2026-11-06. Seed 2026110607.

Scope and timing
This plan is written before calculation of the additional quintile, Precision@K and NDCG@K summaries. RPE1 outcomes and the original UPR effects/selection analyses have already been examined. This is an explicitly retrospective robustness extension of the current manuscript, not new independent validation, prospective evaluation, or a retrospectively preregistered original analysis. Existing five-source and targeted RPE1 gene universes, own-target correction, metadata eligibility, source rankings and comparator lists are unchanged. No RPE1 outcome will select a gene set, ranking, threshold, model, weight, K, covariate or inferential family. No manuscript edits are part of this analysis.

Inputs and cohorts
Primary effects are the previously locked source K562 and RPE1 signed UPR delta-Z values, using the targeted 6,780 measured-gene denominator and 90 UPR members with own-member numerator exclusion and target-specific control SD. The primary cohort remains all 438 metadata-eligible genes. Uniform complete-covariate 370-gene and stricter RPE1-GEM-support 390-gene populations are fixed sensitivities. Source and validation remain contexts from the same Replogle study. Archived source/ranking lists, matching tables, matched-control summaries, per-GEM effects, 2,000 shared-GEM wild draws, exact target-denominator effects, and 200 equal-100 target-cell draws will be reused; no raw-outcome-driven refit or new QC gate will be introduced. All input checksums will be recorded before the analysis script reads effect values.

Source quintiles
Within each fixed cohort, sort genes by increasing K562 signed UPR effect, breaking exact ties by ascending gene symbol. Divide the resulting source-only ordering into five consecutive groups with sizes differing by at most one: quotient floor(N/5), with the first N modulo 5 groups receiving one extra gene. For N=438, sizes are 88/88/88/87/87. These groups are fixed when validation draws are evaluated. For each group report source boundaries, gene identities, mean/median RPE1 effect, number/fraction RPE1 d>0, and source/RPE1 sign concordance. Zero has sign zero and is not positive; concordance requires identical signs, with zero/zero listed separately.
Report Q5-minus-Q1 mean and median contrasts; the linear OLS coefficient of RPE1 effect on integer quintile 1..5; Kendall tau-b between quintile and RPE1 effect as the ordered-distribution trend; and source-quintile mean profile. Use two-sided inference, allowing nonmonotonic or inverse results. A five-point equal-group-weight increasing isotonic fit to group means is descriptive only; report its fitted means and deviations, without treating an enforced monotonic fit as evidence of monotonicity. A monotonic profile is supported only to the extent that observed estimates, trend uncertainty and adjacent differences support it. Isotonic regression is not a prediction model and is never fitted for candidate ranking.

Frozen rankings and lists
Use the CURRENT source-frozen agreement-weighted UPR priority and positive-UPR-effect rankings, plus original source-expression, essentiality, generic three-short-module magnitude, source-count and annotation-derived rankings. Use their original deterministic order and original Top10/Top20 membership; do not optimize K or reorder using validation. The annotation comparator remains supplementary and tie-limited. Use the same 100 source-expression/detection-matched random-module rankings and their originally fixed own-member correction. Comparisons between named methods use the common 370-gene complete-covariate rankings and their fixed Top10/Top20 lists. Also describe current UPR-priority/raw-UPR lists on 438 and all 100 original null rankings on 438. For the 390-gene sensitivity, a literal frozen TopK endpoint is estimable only when every member of that original list is retained; otherwise report coverage and NA for that literal endpoint, with an explicitly labeled retained-subset summary if useful. Do not silently drop or refill a TopK list. Exact-denominator and equal-cell checks retain the original source rankings; recomputed source exact effects do not create new lists.

Utility endpoints
For K in {10,20}, Precision@K is the fraction of fixed selected genes with signed RPE1 d>0. This fixed biological sign criterion is not an optimized RPE1 quantile. Report cohort positive prevalence and precision difference/ratio relative to that prevalence, and positive-sign difference/ratio versus the previously frozen matched background (five per selected role). Zero denominators yield NA ratios, never ad hoc pseudocounts. Also report fixed TopK mean/median signed effect and signed/absolute-effect matched gaps as context.
NDCG@K uses relevance r_g=max(d_RPE1,g,0) with no exponential, rank, quantile or nonlinear transformation. DCG=sum_{j=1..K} r_{gene_j}/log2(j+1). IDCG uses the K largest r_g among the same evaluation cohort with the same discounts; if IDCG=0, NDCG is NA. The ideal RPE1 ordering is an explicitly descriptive normalization/upper bound, never an available selection rule or validated comparator. Report NDCG for every frozen method and fixed null module, with denominators and gene coverage.
Report source/RPE1 sign concordance for all genes and for each fixed TopK list, showing source positive/negative and validation positive/negative counts. For priority lists positivity and sign concordance may coincide because source priorities are positive; state this dependence rather than implying independent validation evidence.
Uniform random-ranking references use 10,000 independently permuted complete gene orderings per cohort, fixed seed, taking nested first10/20. Report expected prevalence/mean effects and reference quantiles for Precision/NDCG; these are computational controls, not biological replicates. For the 100 fixed expression/detection-matched random-module rankings report metric distributions, UPR empirical percentile and finite-null upper-tail fraction (1 + number null >= real)/(1 + number finite null). These finite-control fractions are descriptive calibrations, not gene-permutation P values. All100 null availability/coverage will be preserved, including any unavailable matched-background endpoint.

Uncertainty, dependence and testing
Use 2,000 whole-gene bootstrap draws shared across all methods within a cohort, with one multinomial gene multiplicity per draw. Fixed list membership and rank positions do not change. Means/medians, quintile trends and Precision use these gene weights and actual weighted denominators; zero-weight list draws are recorded as invalid. Overlapping selected/background genes receive the same draw weight. Fixed-matched-set bootstrap is additionally retained for literal TopK-versus-background contrasts and reports its distinct conditional unit. For NDCG method DIFFERENCES, whole-gene bootstrap applies shared multiplicity to the linear per-gene discounted-gain difference and divides by the observed same-cohort IDCG, explicitly conditioning on that observed normalizer; no individual-NDCG population interval is inferred from this conditional linear calculation. Shared-GEM wild draws recompute signed effects, signs, relevance, IDCG and all metrics using fixed source quintiles/rankings/matches, providing a separate technical conditional sensitivity. Whole-gene and technical intervals are not independent biological replication or full source-selection uncertainty. Equal100 draws are sampling-sensitivity distributions rather than confidence intervals. Do not interpret a negative or uncertain added-score advantage as equivalence.
For the primary permutation tests, permute the RPE1 signed-effect vector among genes within the original fixed source-expression-by-count strata, using 10,000 shared permutation draws; missing expression remains its original unknown stratum. Recompute the relevant statistics with fixed source groups/ranks. For pairwise method differences retain overlap exactly through the same permuted effect vector. IDCG is invariant to within-cohort outcome permutations. Use two-sided Monte Carlo P=(1+number |T_perm|>=|T_observed|)/(10001), with numerical equality tolerance 1e-12. A zero observed/permutation statistic returns P=1. For scalar differences whose permutation-null mean is not zero, center observed and permutation statistics on the permutation mean before the two-sided tail; record both raw and centered statistics.

Single prespecified inferential family (seven tests, joint Holm correction)
1. All438 Q5-minus-Q1 mean signed RPE1 UPR effect.
2. All438 Kendall tau-b ordered quintile trend.
3. All438 linear ordinal-quintile coefficient.
4. On370: UPR-priority minus raw-UPR Precision@10.
5. On370: UPR-priority minus raw-UPR Precision@20.
6. On370: UPR-priority minus raw-UPR NDCG@10.
7. On370: UPR-priority minus raw-UPR NDCG@20.
All seven remain in the family even for identical lists or a zero statistic. All other named-comparator, random-ranking, null-module, median, adjacent-quintile, sign-concordance, background-enrichment and sensitivity summaries are explicitly descriptive with conditional intervals and coverage, not additional uncorrected confirmatory significance tests. No result will be promoted to a new primary endpoint after seeing these values. Existing original Holm3 and exploratory48 analyses remain unchanged and distinct.
