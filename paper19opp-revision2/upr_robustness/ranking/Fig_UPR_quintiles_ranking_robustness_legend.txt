Source-UPR quintile gradients and fixed ranking utility in previously evaluated RPE1 data.
a, The 438 metadata-eligible genes grouped into balanced quintiles using signed source K562 UPR effect, with exact ties broken by gene symbol; group sizes are 88, 88, 88, 87 and 87. Grey points are genes; blue points and lines show observed RPE1 means, bars are 95% whole-gene bootstrap intervals, and short black segments denote medians. Means increase from Q1 through Q4 but decrease in Q5; a positive overall trend does not establish strict monotonicity. Quintile membership remains fixed in every resampling draw.
b, Positive RPE1 signs (d > 0) and 95% whole-gene intervals in the same 438-gene population; labels give positive/total gene counts. The dotted line denotes cohort positive prevalence. This fixed sign endpoint does not impose a minimum biologically meaningful effect or measure functional activation.
c,d, Fixed Top 10 (filled blue) and Top 20 (open black) source rankings on the common 370-gene comparator population. Precision is the proportion with signed RPE1 effect > 0. NDCG uses linear relevance max(d, 0) and standard log2 rank discounts; validation-ideal ordering is only a descriptive normalization. Named-method points are observed values for the fixed lists. Paired whole-gene and shared-GEM intervals, together with the joint Holm7 tests, are provided in the accompanying tables. Bars on computational reference rows instead show central 95% distributions across 100 fixed expression/detection-matched random-module lists or 10,000 uniform random orderings; they are not biological confidence intervals. The agreement-weighted and raw-UPR Top 10 lists contain the same genes, but their internal source order differs and therefore gives different NDCG.
All analyses are retrospective robustness extensions: RPE1 outcomes had already been evaluated. RPE1 is a held-out cellular context in the same published study as K562. The locked joint Holm7 family covers three all-gene quintile tests and four priority-minus-raw-UPR utility differences; none of the latter differences demonstrates a weighting advantage after correction. Source rankings, K, sign threshold, gene universe and null memberships were unchanged. Whole-gene intervals condition on fixed source groups; technical GEM, equal-cell and exact-denominator sensitivities are reported separately.
