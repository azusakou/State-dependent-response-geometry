Numbers in row labels are measured readout members. Asterisks indicate BH q<0.05 across all sixteen full/disjoint/unique readouts in five comparisons (80 tests); all eighty results are retained in source data. b, Observed correlation relative to one hundred random readouts matched for exact measured size and source-control expression/detection. Values are finite-control upper-tail fractions, (1+number of null correlations at least as large as the real correlation)/101, not corrected hypothesis-test P values. Both real and null scores in this panel use the same fixed 6,780-gene normalization denominator, own-member exclusion and target-specific control SD. c, Correlations for full and Hallmark-disjoint Reactome and GO UPR definitions, with 95% intervals from 4,999 whole-gene bootstrap draws. Panels a,c use exact removal of raw targeted-gene counts from the normalization denominator and remove the target from the numerator when applicable, in both treated and matched-control cells. The disjoint variants exclude the complete canonical Hallmark membership after approved-symbol mapping. Circles are filled for full and open for disjoint definitions. These analyses use already-examined outcomes and constitute specification-limited robustness, not new independent validation. Annotated RNA summaries do not measure ERAD/ISR biochemical activity or canonical UPR activation. Gene-bootstrap intervals are conditional on the measured target panels and do not supply independent culture replication. The lack of exceptional RPE1 null calibration and the failed disjoint Reactome definition limit readout-specific generalization.
