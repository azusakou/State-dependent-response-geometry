UPR response ordering across independent perturbation studies. a,b, Source Replogle K562 and independent Adamson K562 (a; 30 genes) or untreated Frangieh melanoma (b; 19 genes) UPR shifts, standardized to the respective non-targeting-control standard deviation. Every eligible gene is shown; labels identify the six largest absolute effects in either context. Dashed lines mark zero. Spearman correlations are accompanied by 95% intervals from 2,000 whole-gene bootstrap draws and BH-adjusted q values from 10,000 two-sided whole-gene permutations across the full 48-test family. c, All 12 prespecified molecular readouts in four external contexts: Adamson K562 (30 genes), untreated melanoma (19), IFNg-treated melanoma (19), and melanoma co-culture (17). Asterisks indicate BH q<0.05 across all 48 tests. d, UPR correlations before and after exact removal of the perturbed gene from the UPR numerator when present and from the 7,848-gene normalization denominator when measured, rescoring treated and matched non-targeting cells and recomputing target-specific control standard deviations. Error bars are 95% whole-gene bootstrap intervals; original and corrected estimates are paired within each context. The corrected four UPR tests replace the original four UPR tests in a separate 48-test sensitivity family. These analyses examine ordinal program responses after the original aggregate priority failed external validation. The UPR follow-up is exploratory, and does not establish cross-context amplitude equivalence, a mechanism, biological replication, or a difference between external contexts. Source Data are provided in the accompanying scatter, all-48-association and composition-sensitivity tables.
