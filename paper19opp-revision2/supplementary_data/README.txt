Supplementary Data

All tables are tab-separated UTF-8. Files ending in .gz use gzip compression.
NA denotes an unavailable or unevaluable value; it is not a biological zero.

task_catalogue: 3,554 source conditions, including exclusions. Filter status =
eligible for the retained 705 tasks. The ten agreement-selected MultiPerturb
tasks are excluded by the non-targeting-construct criterion. Source-specific
scales and independent units are defined in Supplementary Notes S1-S4.

gene_evidence: all 9,894 genes, current specificity-aware classes, source support
and assay availability. For compatibility with the source analysis, the code
causal_regulator denotes the operational perturbation-supported class (389),
state_marker denotes the association-only class (4,128), and
insufficient_causal_evidence denotes insufficient perturbation evidence (5,377).
display_evidence_class gives the manuscript terminology. historical_class records
the agreement-only comparison. These labels do not assert validated causality,
durable cell-fate control or candidate priority. candidate_assessment records
non-evaluability when required assays do not overlap. The Boolean candidate
rule is not a biological negative test. Both-context assay coverage is distinct
from eligible movement in two contexts. Repeated K562 resources are one context.

target_specificity and non_targeting_constructs: 27 sufficiently supported ATRT
targets and 120 overlapping non-targeting construct groups. agreement_pass uses
both median cosine cutoffs >0.10. nt_construct_p is the empirical non-targeting
comparison and nt_construct_fdr is BH-adjusted across the 27 targets. None passes
q<0.05. Construct groups overlap and are not independent biological replicates.
candidate_assay_coverage gives the counts plotted in the assay-coverage panel.

intervention_choice_contexts and intervention_choice_summary: matched-candidate
drug choices from fixed count-level nested-scVI out-of-fold predictions. K and E
refer to kernel transition and elastic net. Regret is the observed objective gap
from the best accepted candidate; lower is better. Outcomes are transcriptional
program tail fractions, not measured death, division or benefit. Candidate sets,
policies and predictions are fixed. The 10,000-draw cell-line-cluster percentile
intervals omit model fitting and policy-selection uncertainty (Note S2, Table S3d).

lineage_simulation_summary, lineage_simulation_paired_bias and
lineage_simulation_replicates: stress-independent logistic-normal intrinsic branch
probabilities observed under different descendant-count/neighbourhood scenarios.
There are 200 latent draws per resource and dispersion, reused across nine
observation scenarios: 1,200 latent-draw datasets and 10,800 scenario records.
The 2.5th/97.5th percentiles are computational simulation ranges, not biological
confidence intervals. null_slopes_at_or_below_observed is a descriptive diagnostic,
not a calibrated test or biological P value. See Note S6 for the generator,
fixed covariates, clone sharing and interpretation.

Source data were generated by the investigators cited in the manuscript.
Source-derived inputs retain their original reuse terms. Full source-analysis
code and source terms: https://github.com/azusakou/State-dependent-response-geometry
Additional executable analyses and prepared inputs are provided separately in
the accompanying reproducibility archive.

Common-score transfer and validation data

The subdirectories below contain the final numerical data for the common-score
analyses. table_index.tsv gives each file's row count, column count and purpose.
No cell-level matrices, prediction caches, software logs or previews are included.

measurement/: the fixed 7,848-gene measurement universe, twelve signed score
memberships, measured-gene coverage and source/sample accounting. The targeted
K562-to-RPE1 follow-up uses a separately fixed 6,780-gene universe, ninety UPR
members and one hundred source-expression/detection-matched random modules;
the RPE1-prefixed files and all105-axis table specify that measurement. The
five-study universe and targeted follow-up universe must not be interchanged.

study_transfer/: study-held-out response errors, per-axis errors, paired cluster
contrasts, and the ten fixed random-cell-partition summaries. The first partition
is primary. Errors compare identical eligible units. Contexts/interventions recur
across observations, and conditional cluster intervals are not between-study
biological-replicate intervals.

external_validation/: the original source-frozen aggregate-priority gene lists,
all eligible gene responses, primary comparisons, simple baselines and declared
sensitivities. These candidate tests did not validate the aggregate score.
exploratory_axis_conservation.tsv reports all twelve axes in Adamson K562 and
three Frangieh melanoma contexts, corrected together across 48 tests. Exact UPR
composition controls, context-paired contrasts, covariate/support checks and
response-vector baselines retain their full comparison populations. The UPR
follow-up was selected from this exploratory family. Correlation concerns gene
ordering, not calibrated amplitude or a functional fate outcome.

rpe1_validation/: the 438-gene source-frozen K562-to-RPE1 holdout, all target and
control support, source covariates, source rankings, frozen background matches,
the three Holm-adjusted primary endpoints, all105 validation readouts, comparator
utility, and all100 random-module results including non-estimable lists. Top10
is nested within the single Top20 list. The complete-covariate sensitivity uses
370 genes and has its own fixed rankings/matches; it does not replace the primary
cohort. complete_covariate_null100_list_membership contains these sensitivity
ranks, while the full438-gene null table supplies unchanged source effects/scores.

UPR_priority is max(signed source UPR effect,0) times the fraction of supported
source GEMs with a positive contrast. raw_UPR ranks positive source UPR effect;
it is not an absolute-effect ranking. deltaZ is a matched-GEM contrast divided
by the relevant global control SD. Primary numerator scores exclude the targeted
member when present, in treated and control cells. exact_denominator files also
exclude raw target counts from the normalizing library and recompute every log
term and control standardization. Frozen rankings/matches remain unchanged.

The primary gene/set bootstrap intervals are conditional on the measured panels.
Shared-GEM wild and crossed intervals quantify technical sensitivity, not culture
replication. Equal100 ranges are from 200 without-replacement draws of 100 target
cells per gene in both contexts; original matched-GEM controls and target-specific
control SDs remain fixed. These ranges are not gene-bootstrap confidence intervals.
Signed/absolute and readout-member tables are descriptive diagnostics on the fixed
lists; their absolute-effect intervals do not establish greater response magnitude.
The full primary cohort has only sixteen evaluable matched-null lists. The uniform
370-gene sensitivity has all100; unavailable lists are never counted as negatives.
RPE1 is a held-out context from the Replogle study, not an independent study.

lineage_prediction/: observed parent-state/descendant-response prediction in the
independent Schaff experiment, with primary/stricter support counts, all comparator
errors, and paired full-refitting lineage-bootstrap contrasts. Measurements use
the same 7,848-gene universe and twelve score memberships. Lineages, not sampled
cells, define held-out folds and bootstrap units.

figure_source_data/: the exact values plotted in the final common-score, UPR,
RPE1, original failed-priority and independent-lineage figures, with panel names
retained for direct comparison to the figures. Where plotted data are selected
from a complete table above, the full table remains the inferential denominator.

Source data and reuse terms are described by the cited original studies. The
full reproducibility archive preserves code, immutable source/ranking locks,
cell/prediction inputs and software records separately from this submission data.
